Who is the Leader

Dr Raphael Kamanga
Director and Lead Consultant

Dr Raphael Kamanga is a dynamic business and technology leader with over 30 years’ working experience across various sectors including ICT, financial services, telecommunications, fintech and consulting. He has worked at executive level for leading South African organisations for over 20 years. He is multiskilled, with expertise in strategy development & implementation, IT Service Management, digital transformation, ICT infrastructure and governance. He has worked with clients and partners across the globe, including Africa, Asia, Europe and North America regions. He holds an MSc Information Systems from Leeds Beckett University( UK) and a PhD Information Systems from University of South Africa. He has acquired professional certifications in Microsoft, Cisco, ITIL and X-Road.
